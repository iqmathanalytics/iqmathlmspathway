**Hour 4 (Business + Simple Technical Understanding)**

**How Does RAG Actually Work?**

Now explain technology.

Only conceptual.

**Topics**

Step 1

Company uploads documents

↓

Step 2

AI stores document knowledge

↓

Step 3

User asks question

↓

Step 4

Relevant content is found

↓

Step 5

AI answers

Now introduce terms.

Very simply.

Embeddings

AI converts meaning into searchable information.

Chunking

Large documents are divided into small sections.

Vector Database

Smart document library.

Similarity Search

Finds the most relevant information.

LangChain

Connects AI with documents.

FAISS / ChromaDB

Tools that store document knowledge.

No coding.

No implementation.

**Demonstration**

Show a very simple architecture diagram.

Students only need the concept.
