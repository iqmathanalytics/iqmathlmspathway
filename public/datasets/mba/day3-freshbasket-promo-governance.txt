FRESHBASKET SUPERMARKET — CATEGORY & PROMO GOVERNANCE BRIEF
Document ID: FB-CAT-PROMO-2026-02 | Training brief

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1. PURPOSE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Rules for launching or changing promotional offers so shelf tags,
app banners, and till prices match — protecting margin and customer trust.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2. PROMO TYPES ALLOWED
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
A) Temporary price cut (max 14 days unless Category Head extends)
B) Bundle / multi-buy (e.g., 2 for ₹100)
C) Weekend cut (Friday 00:00 to Sunday 23:59 local store time)
D) App-only offer (must be enforced at till via barcode / offer code)

Flash “lightning” style promises below cost require Finance + Category dual sign-off.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3. APPROVAL LADDER
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Depth 0–5% off list: Category Manager
Depth 6–12% off list: Category Head
Depth >12% or private-label launch promo: Category Head + Finance Partner
Any national campaign creative that mentions a % off: Marketing + Category Joint approval

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4. MANDATORY CONTROLS BEFORE GO-LIVE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1) Shelf tag template checked against till price in POS test store
2) Offer start/end dates entered in promo calendar
3) Store briefing note sent 24 hours before go-live
4) “If till price ≠ tag price, do not argue — call Duty Manager” instruction posted

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5. HARD STOPS (DO NOT LAUNCH)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
• Promo that cannot be scanned / applied at till
• Promo overlapping another active deep cut on same SKU without stock cover
• Claims of “lowest price in city” without documented competitor check date
• Permanent price reduction disguised as a “weekend cut”

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6. PRIVATE-LABEL MILK PILOT NOTES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
FreshBasket Farm Dairy Milk 1L pilot clusters: West (Mumbai) and South (Bengaluru).
Guardrail: do not fund national awareness ads until 4 weeks of on-shelf availability ≥ 95%
on Sundays in pilot stores (measurement owner: Supply + Store Ops).

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
7. ESCALATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Customer “fake discount” complaints → Store Ops logs ticket + Marketing reviews creative
within 48 hours. Repeat till mismatches in same store → Area Manager audit within 7 days.
