1. WHO WE ARE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
FreshBasket is a multi-store grocery retailer operating across India
with a focus on everyday essentials, fresh produce, dairy, and
personal care. Our promise to shoppers: fresh products, fair prices,
and reliable neighbourhood stores.

Founded: 2014
Headquarters: Mumbai, India
Store count (FY2025/26 operating view): 48 stores
Presence: West · South · North · East metro clusters
Formats: Supermarket (larger footprint) and Express (compact urban)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2. WHAT WE SELL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Primary categories:
• Dairy — milk, curd, butter, and chilled essentials
• Fresh Produce — vegetables and fruits replenished daily in A-class stores
• Grocery staples — atta, dals, oils, packaged foods
• Beverages — soft drinks, juices, tea
• Personal Care — soaps, shampoo, hygiene essentials

Private-label direction (pilot): FreshBasket Farm Dairy Milk 1L
Target clusters for pilot: West (Mumbai) and South (Bengaluru)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3. HOW WE WIN (STATED STRENGTHS)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
• Freshness reputation in produce (especially morning loads)
• Neighbourhood trust vs pure dark-store models
• Growing personal-care assortment in metro stores
• Store staff recovery stories after billing errors (training priority)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4. CURRENT BUSINESS PRESSURES (BOARD VIEW)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
• Same-store softness in parts of South; competition from quick-commerce
• Promo intensity in Personal Care and Dairy pressuring margin
• Sunday peak queues and occasional dairy / produce stockouts
• Shopper complaints about offers not applying at the till
• Need for tighter replenishment and clearer promo governance

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5. CUSTOMER & EXPERIENCE NOTES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Common praise themes: produce freshness, helpful staff in some stores
Common complaint themes: Sunday queues, empty dairy on peak hours,
unclear promotional tags, price gaps vs app-based competitors

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6. STRATEGIC PRIORITIES (NEXT 90 DAYS)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1) Protect on-shelf availability for top dairy and produce SKUs on Sundays
2) Fix promo trust (shelf tag = till price)
3) Pilot private-label milk in West + South with clear margin guardrails
4) Run store-level staffing experiments for evening / Sunday billing lanes
5) Build a company knowledge assistant for HR, SOPs, and category briefs
   so managers get answers from approved documents—not guesswork

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
7. RISKS CALLED OUT IN THIS DOCUMENT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
• Competitive catchment pressure near new dark stores
• Margin erosion from unmanaged promotions
• Reputation risk if stockouts and “fake discount” perception continue
• Compliance risk if Sunday overtime documentation is weak
