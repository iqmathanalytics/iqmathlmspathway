FRESHBASKET SUPERMARKET PVT. LTD.
HR Policy Extract — Leave, Working Hours & Remote Guidelines
Document ID: FB-HR-POL-2026-03 | Training extract | Effective: 1 Jan 2026

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1. PURPOSE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
This extract guides store and office employees on leave, working hours,
and limited remote work rules. It applies to full-time employees on
FreshBasket payroll. Contract workers follow the vendor agreement
(not covered in this extract).

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2. WORKING HOURS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Standard store roster: up to 9 hours per shift including breaks,
as scheduled by the Store Manager.
Peak stores may roster Sunday evening billing support with prior notice.
Compulsory weekly offs follow the published store roster.
Office staff (HQ): Monday–Friday, 9:30–18:30 IST unless project duty applies.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3. LEAVE TYPES (SUMMARY)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Casual Leave (CL): 8 days per calendar year.
Sick Leave (SL): 10 days per calendar year (medical note required after 2 consecutive days).
Earned Leave (EL): 15 days per calendar year after completing 12 months of continuous service.
Maternity / Paternity: as per applicable law; HR must be notified with documentation.

Leave applications must be submitted in the HR portal at least 3 working days
in advance for CL/EL (except sudden SL emergencies).

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4. APPROVAL AUTHORITY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Store employees: Store Manager approves CL/SL; Area Manager approval if leave > 3 days.
HQ employees: Reporting Manager approves; HR Business Partner reviews EL encashment.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5. SUNDAY OVERTIME
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Sunday overtime for store staff is allowed only with Store Manager written approval
in the roster system BEFORE the shift starts.
Unauthorised overtime will not be compensated.
All Sunday OT entries must be documented for labour-compliance checks.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6. REMOTE WORK
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Store roles are on-site by nature; permanent remote work is not available for cashiers,
floor supervisors, or replenishment staff.
HQ roles may get “Work From Home” for up to 4 days per month with Reporting Manager approval,
subject to meeting attendance and data-security rules (no customer PII on personal devices).

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
7. PROMOTION RULES (HIGH LEVEL)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Promotion reviews occur twice a year (April cycle and October cycle).
Eligibility requires: minimum 12 months in current role AND no active disciplinary case
AND manager recommendation recorded in HR portal.
Final grade approval sits with HR Head for Band-3 and above.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
8. WHAT THIS DOCUMENT DOES NOT COVER
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Salary structures, gratuity calculations, vendor staffing contracts,
and legal opinions on state-specific labour nuances — contact HR Business Partner.

Training note: Simplified for LMS labs. Not a substitute for full legal counsel.
